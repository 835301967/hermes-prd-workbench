# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical ideas, not personal attributes. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish personal information or post private credentials/documents.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through [835301967@qq.com](mailto:835301967@qq.com), the maintainer’s designated private conduct contact. Safety reports use SECURITY.md. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the applicable rule without redistributing private material.
